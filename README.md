Original by mrsterner.

Modified specifically for version 26.1.2

Also removed blacksmith heal task and constrained cleric heal task to guards only as my server runs another villager task mod for the same purpose.

Added the ability for Guard's to use Bows in addition to Crossbows

Constrained Offhand slot to only shields and consumable.

Changed aggression and priority behaviors
- Bow Guards now back up to capitalize on the mobility of a bow
- Crossbow Guards commit more to shooting their targets, but kick enemies back aggressively between racking their arrows and actually shooting.
- - Tuned Crossbow Guard accuracy to be more in line with that of Pillagers for both consistency, and also to address the fact that Crossbow Guards struggled to dispatch basic enemies.
- Melee Guards are far more aggressive, and are less likely to turtle behind a shield indefinitely. They will shield from a distance against ranged foes, and intermittently block damage in melee range, but will otherwise close the distance to attack with their weapons.

So overall Guards are significantly more aggressive, and effective at dispatching hostiles while still being a balanced armed force for villages.

Also removed the weird decision to have an eye of ender effect when Guard's use consumables.
